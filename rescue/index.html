USE WITHOUT NABLESS

Use your Vault if Nabless is down or gone.

Your Vault lives on the network, not on our servers. This page talks to the network directly. It asks nothing of the Nabless service.

When to use this

Use it when the app cannot reach Nabless, or if Nabless has shut down. If the app works, use the app: it is easier, and it warns you about risky payments.

What you need

  • Your Vault file. You save it from the app while Nabless is working.
  • Your passkey, on this device.
  • Someone to carry your transaction to the network: a wallet in this browser, or a bundler address.

What it can do

Show your balances and limits. Send coins and tokens. Freeze your Vault and ask to unfreeze it. Cancel a payment that is waiting, or send it on when its wait is over. Start and cancel a full withdrawal.

Get you back in with your recovery kit and your backup key, if you have both. Freeze your Vault or stop a recovery with your backup key.

What it cannot do

It cannot skip your Vault's waits or limits: the network enforces them. It does not warn you about risky addresses, send emails or show your history. It cannot act for a Shared Vault, send NFTs, or change limits or contacts. Without a backup key it cannot recover a lost passkey.

Network fees

Your Vault pays its own network fee from its balance. Someone must still carry the transaction to the network and pay first. Your Vault pays them back at once. You see the most a fee can be before you approve.

If nabless.com itself is gone

Your passkey only works on nabless.com. That is what stops a fake copy of this page from using it. It also means this: if the address nabless.com is gone for good, your passkey cannot approve anything, here or anywhere else. Your guardians' passkeys are tied to nabless.com in the same way.

Your recovery kit and your backup key are ordinary keys, so they are not tied to any site. Together they are the two approvals a recovery needs. With both you can get back in from a copy of this page at any address: the page makes a new passkey for the address it is on, and your Vault obeys it once its safety wait is over. With the kit alone you cannot, because the second approval would have to come from a guardian's passkey.

What still works with no passkey at all: anyone can send on a payment whose wait is over, and anyone can finish an unfreeze or a recovery that was already started.

This page needs no Nabless server, only this address. If Nabless ever says it is closing, move your money out while nabless.com still opens.

Running this page yourself

This page can be built as a folder of plain files that needs no server of ours. You, or anyone, can put that folder on any web address, or open it on your own computer. It then talks only to the network.

A passkey only works at the address it was made on. On a copy at another address, your old passkey does nothing. Use your recovery kit and your backup key below to put a new passkey on your Vault, and keep using that copy at that same address.

Only use a copy you trust. A false copy cannot move your money without your approvals, but it could ask for your kit, your backup key and their passwords, and use them. Never type them into a page you did not get from a source you trust.
